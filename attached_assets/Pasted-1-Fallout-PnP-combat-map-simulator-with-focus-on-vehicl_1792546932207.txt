1. Fallout PnP combat map simulator with focus on vehicle combat. Either included in the PnP app or as it's own app.  Fallout PnP driving manouvers on a a simulated grid. Include ability to make vehicles a specific size (how much grid they occupy) and ability to upload custom images, including transparent GIFs, as skins for vehicles. Include custom grid scaling, the option for hexagonal or square grids, and ability to upload custom scalable terrain maps representing the ground. Include vehicle library for quick spawning, it will have all the vehicle stats listed in the manual. Include options for building custom vehicles. We will also need a list of customizable pilots, which can be as simple as a character with a portrait and assigning them a pilot skill from 1-200. When vehicles are spawned in they will ask to assign a "pilot" who will then fill the slot on the vehicle. When selecting the vehicle there will be the option to move/rotate it for free and the normal movement and manouver options. When selecting a manouver option it will present the user with manouver difficulty vs the assigned pilot's skill and show the possible movement options (swerving left vs swerving right) as selectable graphics on the grid. When the direction of the maneuver is selected give the option to roll against (under) pilot skill minus maneuver difficulty on the percentile dice. Also give the option to manually succeed or fail the manouver in case the players are rolling real dice. When the user succeeds on their piloting check the manouver will automatically move and rotate the vehicle to it's new position. If it's possible make this a smooth animated transition to simulate driving/vehicle movement. Optionally, add a small particle effect that represents dust/dirt being kicked up from behind the vehicle, with the option to customize dust/dirt color in map settings. On the map add the option to add stickers to serve as obstacles. They will be a simple overlay in the form of an uploadable image, including transparent GIFs, which will be scalable and rotatable. When looking the the map, the user will see an "add obstacles" button which will put them into the mode that allows them to upload and place stickers. When editing, already placed stickers should be selectable for adjusting to deleting.
