The visualizer will be a grid that the players can have tokens of their characters and NPCs on and move them.  It will be a hex-based grid, with the option to change it to squares. Underneath. Underneath the grid will be the image representing the ground, which users will be able to upload custom images if they want. The plan is to have this visualizer integrate with the rest of the systems and play like a game, so it's basically an optional way to do the combat, and it will stay persistent with all the things that are already happening in the combat such as initiative. In the case of icons for vehicles, they will take up multiple hexes and also have spots in to plug in character icons so that they can physically be in the vehicle moving around on the map. The standee adjustment system will include the option to replace the standee with an stl model.
