---
name: Fallout PnP helmet gas ratings
description: How the manual's two-part helmet gas ratings map to the app's single gas-resistance field.
---

Environmental and Power Armor Helmets grant +100 Gas Resistance. The second printed values in `GR 100/10` and `GR 100/20` are intentionally not represented.

**Why:** The armor-effects schema requested for this product has one Gas Resistance modifier, not both parts of the manual's gas rating. Inventing a second meaning or overloading another resistance would be misleading.

**How to apply:** Keep helmet gas effects at +100 unless the product explicitly adds a separate field and rule for the second gas number. Continue enforcing the printed suit compatibility independently.
