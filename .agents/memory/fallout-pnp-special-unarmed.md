---
name: Fallout PnP Special Unarmed
description: Manual interpretation and a source-of-truth correction for special unarmed techniques.
---

Special unarmed techniques apply to bare hands and weapons in the Unarmed category, including knuckles and power fists, but not to creature Natural attacks. Eligibility uses effective Unarmed skill, including the free +20 tagged bonus.

Armor-piercing techniques ignore the defender's Armor Class for the hit roll only. Damage Threshold and Damage Resistance still mitigate damage normally. A selected technique is its own normal attack and cannot be combined with targeted-shot or burst modes.

**Why:** The manual says these attacks cover weapons that enhance unarmed combat and describes piercing as ignoring armor class. Secondary summaries can conflict with the printed technique table.

**How to apply:** Preserve the category boundary and AC-only piercing behavior. Use the printed pp. 18–19 table as the source for technique values and requirements.
