---
name: Fallout PnP powered weapons
description: Ammunition policy for powered melee and unarmed weapons, including the deliberate Super Sledge extension.
---

Powered melee and unarmed weapons consume their defined ammunition on every attack, including misses. The Super Sledge uses 25 Small Energy Cell charges.

**Why:** The manual explicitly gives ammo to the Punch Gun, Power Fists, Cattle Prods, and Ripper, but does not print a Super Sledge capacity. The user explicitly requested that the Super Sledge take ammunition, so 25 Small Energy Cell charges matches the other powered melee weapons.

**How to apply:** Preserve ammo compatibility for custom melee and unarmed weapons. Do not remove the Super Sledge charge requirement during manual-data audits unless the user reverses this product decision.
