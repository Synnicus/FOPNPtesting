Use the manual's five successful damaging hits per lost armor box. The user explicitly replaced their initial three-hit request with five and confirmed weapons keep whole-box tracking.

**Why:** The user supplied the armor condition chart and confirmed manual rules, rather than a house-rule segmented weapon system.

**How to apply:** DT and DR reductions apply to each piece's original protection; do not compound reductions or degrade AC, natural protection, or unrelated resistances. Preserve fractional protection until existing final damage rounding.

For separate helmets, aimed head/eyes hits wear the valid helmet; other hits wear body armor. An integrated helmet belongs to its suit. This is an explicit app/GM policy, not a claim that the manual specifies separate helmet accounting.

**Why:** The manual discusses a set of armor but does not specify separate-piece hit routing. Double-counting wear on both pieces would invent extra degradation.

**How to apply:** Record wear after the attack resolves with its starting protection. Count per-hit physical damage where available; aggregate area exposures count once, and ongoing exposure wear remains manually GM-adjustable. Generic HP edits, poison, and radiation are not armor hits. Keep per-character item wear when unequipped; different copies of a catalog item within one character remain outside scope.
