---
name: Vehicle criticals and repair
description: Applies shared critical outcomes and manual repair rules to vehicle systems.
---

Vehicle critical hits use the shared critical table against the selected vehicle system. Keep generic damage multipliers and armor bypass, skip character-only injury/status effects, and treat Instant Death as destroying only the selected system; the vehicle itself is destroyed only when its structure reaches zero.

Vehicle repairs take 8 hours and require a Repair check. Success restores 3d10 HP to one damaged, non-destroyed section, capped at its maximum HP. Destroyed sections require replacement. Keep repair attempts with the persistent vehicle instance so results survive campaign saves and imports.

**Why:** The manual does not provide a vehicle-only critical table, while its vehicle rules track section HP independently. Applying human limb or condition effects would invent vehicle rules; applying Instant Death to the selected system preserves the system model. The repair entry gives an 8-hour duration, Repair check, and 3d10 recovery, despite a conflicting later “three hours” typo.

**How to apply:** When resolving vehicle criticals, route damage to the currently selected system and do not apply character injuries. When repairing, use the selected mechanic’s effective Repair skill, record successes and failures, and prevent repairing a destroyed section.
